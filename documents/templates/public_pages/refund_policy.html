Refund Policy

Last Updated: April 2, 2026

Overview

At TeamManager, we strive to provide the best service possible. This Refund Policy outlines the terms and conditions under which refunds may be issued for our subscription services.

Free Trial Period

We offer a 14-day free trial for all new subscriptions. During this period:

  • You can cancel at any time without any charges
  • No credit card is required to start the trial
  • Full access to all features of your selected plan
  • No refund is necessary as no payment has been made

Subscription Refunds

Monthly Subscriptions

For monthly subscription plans:

  • Within 7 days of payment: Full refund available if you're not satisfied with the service
  • After 7 days: No refund will be issued, but you can cancel to prevent future charges
  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle
  • You retain access to the service until the end of the paid period

Annual Subscriptions

For annual subscription plans:

  • Within 30 days of payment: Full refund available minus any usage fees
  • After 30 days: Pro-rated refund may be considered on a case-by-case basis
  • Refunds are calculated based on unused months remaining
  • A processing fee of 10% may apply to annual refunds after 30 days

Non-Refundable Items

The following are not eligible for refunds:

  • Add-on services or features purchased separately
  • Custom development or integration work
  • Training sessions or consulting services already delivered
  • Third-party services or integrations
  • Subscriptions cancelled after the refund eligibility period
  • Accounts terminated due to violation of Terms of Service

Cancellation Process

To cancel your subscription:

  1. Log in to your TeamManager account
  2. Navigate to Settings → Subscription
  3. Click "Cancel Subscription"
  4. Follow the prompts to confirm cancellation
  5. You'll receive a confirmation email

Alternatively, you can contact our support team at contact@teammanager.ng for assistance with cancellation.

Refund Request Process

To request a refund:

  1. Contact our support team at contact@teammanager.ng
  2. Include your account email and subscription details
  3. Provide a brief reason for the refund request
  4. Our team will review your request within 3-5 business days
  5. If approved, refunds are processed within 7-10 business days

Note: Refunds are issued to the original payment method used for the purchase.

Special Circumstances

We understand that exceptional situations may arise. In cases of:

  • Technical issues preventing service access for extended periods
  • Billing errors or duplicate charges
  • Service outages affecting your business operations
  • Other extenuating circumstances

Please contact our support team. We'll review your case individually and work to find a fair resolution.

Upgrades and Downgrades

Upgrades:

  • When upgrading, you'll be charged the prorated difference immediately
  • New features are available immediately upon upgrade
  • Your billing cycle remains the same

Downgrades:

  • Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle
  • No refund is issued for the difference in plan cost
  • You retain access to higher-tier features until the cycle ends

Payment Disputes

If you dispute a charge with your bank or credit card company:

  • Please contact us first to resolve the issue
  • Chargebacks may result in immediate account suspension
  • We'll work with you to resolve billing concerns directly
  • Disputed charges may incur additional processing fees

Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Refund Policy at any time. Changes will be:

  • Posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date
  • Communicated via email to active subscribers
  • Effective immediately upon posting unless otherwise stated
  • Applied to new subscriptions and renewals

Contact Us

If you have questions about our Refund Policy or need assistance with a refund request, please contact us:

Email: contact@teammanager.ng

Support: Contact Support Form

Response Time: Within 24-48 hours
